History & Royal Heritage

Chronicles of the Sultanate

3000 BCE

The Land of Magan

Renowned across the ancient world for high-grade copper manufacturing and frankincense trade with Mesopotamia and Egypt.

1650 CE

The Yaruba Dynasty Era

Expulsion of foreign colonial powers and construction of iconic fortified strongholds including Nizwa Fort.

1749 CE

Establishment of Al Said Dynasty

Imam Ahmad bin Said establishes the Al Said dynasty, laying the groundwork for modern maritime power and diplomatic prestige.

1970 - Present

The Modern Omani Renaissance

Comprehensive modernization under Late Sultan Qaboos bin Said and continuation under His Majesty Sultan Haitham bin Tarik.
